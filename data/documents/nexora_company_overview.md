# Nexora Dynamics Pvt. Ltd.

## Company Overview

Nexora Dynamics Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional technology company headquartered in
Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

The company was founded on 17 February 2018 by Anirudh Mehta and Kavya Rao.

Nexora develops enterprise automation software, internal workflow platforms,
and AI-assisted business applications.

The company's internal employee identifier is called an NDX-ID.

Every employee receives an NDX-ID after completing onboarding.

## Offices

Nexora currently operates from three offices:

1. Bengaluru Technology Centre
2. Pune Operations Centre
3. Hyderabad Engineering Centre

The Bengaluru Technology Centre is located in the fictional Orion Tech Park,
Tower C.

## Departments

The primary departments at Nexora are:

- Engineering
- Product
- Human Resources
- Finance
- Sales
- Customer Success
- Information Security
- Business Operations

## Working Hours

The standard working window is from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM,
Monday through Friday.

Employees receive a one-hour lunch break between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM.

Employees working with teams in different time zones may have modified
working hours approved by their department manager.

## Core Values

Nexora officially defines five core values:

1. Build with Intent
2. Own the Outcome
3. Learn in Public
4. Respect the Clock
5. Leave Systems Better

The phrase "Leave Systems Better" refers specifically to improving internal
processes, documentation, and engineering systems rather than environmental
sustainability.

## Internal Communication

Nexora uses the following internal communication systems:

- OrbitChat for instant messaging
- NexoraHub for internal documentation
- PulseDesk for HR requests
- TrackFlow for engineering work items

HR requests submitted through PulseDesk receive a ticket number beginning
with the prefix "PDSK-".




# Nexora Dynamics Leave Policy

## Effective Date

This policy became effective on 1 April 2026.

## Annual Leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 annual leave days per calendar year.

Annual leave is credited monthly at the rate of 2 days per month.

Employees joining after the first month of a calendar year receive
prorated annual leave.

## Casual Leave

Employees receive 6 casual leave days per calendar year.

Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the following calendar year.

Unused casual leave automatically expires on 31 December.

## Wellness Leave

Nexora provides 4 wellness leave days per calendar year.

Wellness leave may be used without providing a medical certificate.

Employees may use wellness leave for physical or mental rest.

Wellness leave cannot be converted into cash.

## Carry Forward

A maximum of 8 unused annual leave days may be carried forward
to the following calendar year.

Casual leave and wellness leave cannot be carried forward.

## Leave Approval

Leave of up to 2 consecutive working days can be approved by the
employee's direct manager.

Leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days requires approval from
both the direct manager and the department head.

## Emergency Leave

In an emergency, an employee may notify their manager after the leave
has started.

The employee must submit the leave request through PulseDesk within
2 working days after returning to work.

## Probation

Employees on probation receive annual leave according to the same
monthly accrual system as confirmed employees.

However, leave exceeding 3 consecutive working days during probation
requires HR approval.

## Leave During Notice Period

Employees serving their notice period may use accumulated annual leave
only with written approval from their manager and HR.

Wellness leave may still be used during the notice period without
manager approval, provided the employee follows the normal PulseDesk
submission process.




# Nexora Dynamics Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid Work Model

Nexora follows a structured hybrid working model.

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

Employees must work from their assigned office at least 2 days per week
unless an exception has been approved.

## Remote Work Days

Remote work days must normally be selected before the beginning of
each working week.

Employees must update their working location in NexoraHub.

## Temporary Remote Work

Employees may request temporary full-week remote work for personal
circumstances.

Such requests require approval from the employee's department head.

Temporary full-week remote work cannot exceed 10 consecutive working days.

## Internet Requirement

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a stable
internet connection.

Nexora does not reimburse standard home internet expenses.

## Security

Employees must connect to company systems using the Nexora Secure Access
Gateway.

Company laptops must not be connected to public or unsecured Wi-Fi
networks while accessing confidential company systems.

## Remote Work During Travel

Employees may not work remotely from outside India without written approval
from Information Security and Human Resources.

Such approval must be obtained before the employee begins working from
the foreign location.

## Office Attendance

Employees who fail to meet the minimum two-day office requirement for
three consecutive weeks may receive an automated attendance review notice.

The notice is generated by NexoraHub.


# Nexora Dynamics Employee Benefits

## Meal Allowance

Employees receive a monthly meal allowance of INR 2,400.

The allowance is loaded onto the Nexora Meal Card on the first working
day of every month.

Unused balance expires at the end of the month.

## Internet Allowance

Employees working in eligible remote roles receive an internet allowance
of INR 800 per month.

The allowance is paid along with the monthly salary.

## Learning Allowance

Every full-time employee receives an annual learning allowance of
INR 18,000.

The allowance can be used for:

- Technical courses
- Professional certifications
- Conference tickets
- Technical books

The learning allowance cannot be used to purchase personal electronic
devices.

## Certification Reimbursement

Employees who complete an approved professional certification may claim
reimbursement for the examination fee.

The employee must submit the reimbursement request within 30 days
of receiving the certification result.

## Health Check-up

Nexora provides one preventive health check-up reimbursement of up to
INR 5,000 per employee per financial year.

Unused health check-up reimbursement cannot be carried forward.

## Relocation Support

Employees relocating between Nexora offices may receive relocation support
of up to INR 35,000.

Relocation support requires approval from HR before the relocation begins.

## Employee Referral

Employees receive INR 12,000 for a successful engineering referral.

The referred candidate must complete 90 days of employment before the
referral payment becomes payable.

Contractors and interns are not eligible for referral bonuses.

# Nexora Performance Review Policy

## Review Cycle

Nexora conducts two formal performance reviews every year.

The review periods are:

1. January to June
2. July to December

## Review Components

Performance reviews consider four areas:

- Delivery
- Technical or functional capability
- Collaboration
- Ownership

Each category is evaluated using a five-level scale.

The levels are:

1. Developing
2. Progressing
3. Consistent
4. Strong
5. Exceptional

## Self Review

Employees must complete their self-review within 7 calendar days
after the review window opens.

## Manager Review

Managers have 10 calendar days after receiving the employee's self-review
to complete their assessment.

## Calibration

Performance ratings are reviewed during a calibration meeting.

Calibration meetings are conducted by department leadership and HR.

## Promotion

Promotion decisions are not based solely on performance ratings.

Promotion consideration may include:

- Scope of responsibility
- Sustained performance
- Role requirements
- Business requirements
- Demonstrated capability

## Performance Improvement Plan

An employee may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
when sustained performance concerns have been documented.

The standard PIP duration is 60 calendar days.

The employee receives documented objectives at the beginning of the PIP.

A manager must conduct at least two formal progress reviews during
the PIP period.


# Nexora Employee Data and Information Security Policy

## Employee Data

Employee records are classified as Confidential Internal Information.

Access to employee records is restricted based on job responsibility.

## HR Systems

PulseDesk stores HR service requests.

NexoraHub stores employee-facing documentation.

Employee payroll information is stored in the internal system called
LedgerCore.

## Access Requests

Employees must not request another employee's payroll information
through PulseDesk.

Managers may access employee information only when required for an
authorized business purpose.

## Employee Export

Employees may request a copy of their own stored profile information
through PulseDesk.

The request must contain the employee's NDX-ID.

HR normally processes such requests within 5 working days.

## Security Incident Reporting

Employees must report suspected exposure of confidential employee data
to Information Security within 4 hours of discovering the incident.

The incident must also be recorded through PulseDesk.

## Password Policy

Nexora employees must change their corporate password every 120 days.

Passwords must not be reused across Nexora corporate systems.

Employees must never share corporate passwords with managers,
colleagues, contractors, or HR personnel.
